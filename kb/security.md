# Security

Files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

Two-factor authentication is available on every plan using an authenticator app or a hardware security key.

SAML single sign-on (SSO) is available on the Team plan only.

Audit logs record sign-ins and sharing events. They are available on the Team plan and are retained for one year.

You choose a data region, United States or European Union, when you create your account. The region cannot be changed later.
